
## Shrimp and Scallop Scampi

##### Ingredients:
| Quantity | Ingredient       |
|----------|------------------|
| 1/2 lb.  | angel hair pasta |
| 1/2 lb.  | bay scallops     |
| 1/2 lb.  | shell-on shrimp  |
| 2 tblsp. | olive oil        |
| 1 cup    | dry white wine   |
| 1        | lemon, juiced    |
| 2 tblsp. | butter           |
| 4 cloves | garlic           |
| 1 tsp.   | salt             |
| 3 tblsp. | parsley, chopped |

##### Preparation:
Boil the pasta according to package directions. Reserve 1/2 cup of the pasta water 
before draining.  Drain and set aside the pasta. 

Peel the shrimp and reserve shells or tails. De-vein if necessary. Pat the scallops 
completely dry. 

While the pasta is boiling, heat a thin layer of oil in a deep skillet over medium-high 
heat. Once the oil is hot, add the dry scallops and sear them on each side until golden 
brown, about 1-2 minutes per side. Remove and set aside. 

Turn heat down to medium-low and melt the butter with the remaining olive oil.  Add the 
garlic and cook until fragrant, about 1 minute. Add white wine, pasta water, shrimp shells, 
salt and red pepper flakes.  Cook until reduced by half. Shrimp shells will turn pink 
and wine will reduce, leaving about half of the liquid or 3/4 cup.

Remove the shrimp tails and add the shrimp.  Cook the shrimp until just barely pink, about 
1 to 2 minutes per side.  Add scallops back into the skillet and stir.  Add lemon juice and
parsley and stir, then remove from heat.  Add pasta into the skillet and and toss to combine
with the sauce. Serve with extra chopped parsley.

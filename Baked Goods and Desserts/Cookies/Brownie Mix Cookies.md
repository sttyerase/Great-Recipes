## Fudge Brownie Mix Cookies

##### Ingredients:

| Quantity       | Ingredients                |
|----------------|----------------------------|
| 1 18.3 oz. box | Fudge Brownie mix          |
| 1/2 cup        | shortening, melted         |
| 1              | large egg                  |
| 1 cup          | semi-sweet chocolate chips |
| 1 tblsp.       | water                      |
|                | flaky salt (optional)      |

##### Preparation:

Preheat oven to 340&deg;. Line 2 large baking sheets with parchment paper.

In a medium bowl with hand mixer, beat together dry brownie mix, melted shortening and egg. 
Add water to help moisten the mix. Fold in chocolate chips.

Place 1-1/2" balls of dough on baking sheets at least 1 inch apart.

Bake until edges are set and centers no longer look wet, approx. 11 min.
